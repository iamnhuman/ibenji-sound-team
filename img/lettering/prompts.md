# Референсы леттеринга

Референсы созданы встроенным ImageGen. На сайте используются пиксельные маски из `scripts/reference-lettering.cjs`.

## Двойной ромб

Use case: logo-brand. Generate one isolated two-line typographic lockup for an existing monochrome diamond-shaped sports emblem. Exact text, centered on two lines: "iBenji" (lowercase i, capital B, lowercase e n j i), then "SOUND TEAM". White lettering on completely flat pure black. The upper line is a bespoke compact 1980s sports-tech wordmark, wide and sturdy, with chiseled diagonal cuts that echo a nested diamond, visually balanced spacing, obvious i dots and a distinctive hooked j. Strongly readable at small size. Make the second line shorter, slim but solid geometric squared-off caps, a single space between words. Crisp square-pixel stair steps at a deliberate fine 8-bit scale with only flat white and black. Keep counters open and each letter separate. Single centered treatment, generous black around it, no emblems, marks, underlines, frames, shadows, gradients, textures, or other text. Landscape artboard. Intended for a game sprite, clear black-and-white silhouettes.

## Три алмаза

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single isolated 8-bit motorsport signature reading exactly "SOUND TEAM", uppercase, on one line. This is the lower caption for an existing Mitsubishi-inspired red three-diamond mark and white IBENJI wordmark. Design original flat white lettering on pure black with crisp square pixel stairs, gently forward-leaning angular letter shapes, chamfered racing stencil corners, even spacing, large open counters, and solid medium-weight strokes readable at only seven or eight pixels high. Make the line wide, roughly 10:1 width to height, and keep the letters distinctly separate. Single centered treatment on a landscape black artboard, no top line, no badge, no underline, no dashes, no shading, no effects, and no additional text.
## Три полосы — нижняя подпись

Use case: logo-brand. Draw one isolated sportswear caption reading exactly "SOUND TEAM" in all caps, on a single centered line. This will sit closely under a bold white 8-bit sportswear wordmark with three rising diagonal stripes. Create an original broad, low and grounded modular pixel letterform, squared octagonal corners, clean full-size counters, slightly cut terminals that echo the stripe angles, with a minimal forward lean. Medium-bold strokes and generous but deliberate tracking, with a clear word space. Wide silhouette approximately 10:1, strong small-size legibility, crisply stepped square pixels. Flat solid white glyphs on absolutely pure black, with black margins. One option only. No logo, upper title, extra text, marks, dots, shadow, underline, shading, texture, or metallic outline.

## Три алмаза — верхний леттеринг

Use case: logo-brand. Generate one isolated wordmark reading exactly "IBENJI", all six letters in uppercase, on a solid pure black landscape artboard. This replaces the large main word in an existing red three-diamond rally emblem. Bespoke retro Japanese motorsport lettering, broad confident uppercase forms, controlled shallow forward rake, sculpted 45-degree diamond-cut terminals, two large clearly separated counters in B, a strong hooked J, and unmistakable initial and final I. Each letter remains separate with deliberate gaps. Reference the geometry of a rally identification plate, with an angular look distinct from a generic squared videogame font. Crisp square-pixel stair steps, moderately fine 8-bit grid, flat pure white letter silhouettes only. Horizontal 4.5:1 proportions for the word, centered with clear black margin. Exact text IBENJI, no other words, emblems, circled marks, colored accents, strokes, outline, bevel shadows, texture, or decoration.
