# Референсы леттеринга

Референсы созданы встроенным ImageGen. На сайте используются пиксельные маски из `scripts/reference-lettering.cjs`.

## Двойной ромб

Use case: logo-brand. Generate one isolated two-line typographic lockup for an existing monochrome diamond-shaped sports emblem. Exact text, centered on two lines: "iBenji" (lowercase i, capital B, lowercase e n j i), then "SOUND TEAM". White lettering on completely flat pure black. The upper line is a bespoke compact 1980s sports-tech wordmark, wide and sturdy, with chiseled diagonal cuts that echo a nested diamond, visually balanced spacing, obvious i dots and a distinctive hooked j. Strongly readable at small size. Make the second line shorter, slim but solid geometric squared-off caps, a single space between words. Crisp square-pixel stair steps at a deliberate fine 8-bit scale with only flat white and black. Keep counters open and each letter separate. Single centered treatment, generous black around it, no emblems, marks, underlines, frames, shadows, gradients, textures, or other text. Landscape artboard. Intended for a game sprite, clear black-and-white silhouettes.

## Три алмаза

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single isolated 8-bit motorsport signature reading exactly "SOUND TEAM", uppercase, on one line. This is the lower caption for an existing Mitsubishi-inspired red three-diamond mark and white IBENJI wordmark. Design original flat white lettering on pure black with crisp square pixel stairs, gently forward-leaning angular letter shapes, chamfered racing stencil corners, even spacing, large open counters, and solid medium-weight strokes readable at only seven or eight pixels high. Make the line wide, roughly 10:1 width to height, and keep the letters distinctly separate. Single centered treatment on a landscape black artboard, no top line, no badge, no underline, no dashes, no shading, no effects, and no additional text.

## Три алмаза — верхний леттеринг

Use case: logo-brand. Generate one isolated wordmark reading exactly "IBENJI", all six letters in uppercase, on a solid pure black landscape artboard. This replaces the large main word in an existing red three-diamond rally emblem. Bespoke retro Japanese motorsport lettering, broad confident uppercase forms, controlled shallow forward rake, sculpted 45-degree diamond-cut terminals, two large clearly separated counters in B, a strong hooked J, and unmistakable initial and final I. Each letter remains separate with deliberate gaps. Reference the geometry of a rally identification plate, with an angular look distinct from a generic squared videogame font. Crisp square-pixel stair steps, moderately fine 8-bit grid, flat pure white letter silhouettes only. Horizontal 4.5:1 proportions for the word, centered with clear black margin. Exact text IBENJI, no other words, emblems, circled marks, colored accents, strokes, outline, bevel shadows, texture, or decoration.

## Синяя орбита

Create a bespoke two-line pixel lettering lockup for a 1990s microprocessor-inspired mark. Show only the exact lowercase word "ibenji", then the exact uppercase phrase "SOUND TEAM" centered beneath on a pure black field. Wide format, clear empty margins. The top word should evoke vintage Intel-era chip design, but custom drawn: wide rounded-square lowercase with horizontally extended counters, subtle optical bevels in the outer silhouette, square dots, a pronounced smooth J descender, consistent rhythm and no generic arcade font. Clear tiny subtitle as a related squared technical sans, generous tracking with a visible word space, approximately one third the height and a similar width. Only solid white artwork over black, hard-edged 1-bit squared pixel art, no texture, gradients, outline, embellishment, symbols, logos, border or extra text. Each word must be spelled exactly.

## Аркадное солнце

Produce original hand-lettered logotype art, NOT a font sample. Exact text "IBENJI" and below "SOUND TEAM". Create a bespoke 1978 arcade cabinet marquee wordmark that feels custom engraved specifically for a stylized three-pronged rising-sun emblem. Broad confident geometry like classic arcade hardware, with unexpected carefully drawn glyphs: two upright I's with asymmetric triangular chamfered ends and no slabs or serifs, a wide B with an upper trapezoidal counter and a larger lower rounded-square counter, E made of three broad horizontal fins with offset right tips, strongly hooked J whose lower stroke bends out like a flare, and a tall N with a sharply sculpted diagonal. Consistent forward 9-degree rake and large intentional negative spaces, readable in six distinct separated letters. Carve a few short negative-space racing notches along the extreme outer corners only. Solid warm-white main face on pitch black. Compact white supporting line SOUND TEAM in an extended thin-but-solid futuristic arcade marquee micro-lettering, generous tracking, shaped as a companion to the main word with flatter oval O, clear word space. Give the two lines plenty of breathing room and black margins. Style: designer-custom Atari cabinet-era sci-fi logotype, Japanese arcade marquee, deliberate small square stair step raster texture. NO generic 5x7 pixel font, plain block slab capitals, outlines, extrusions, icons, shadows, border, noise, extra words, or decorative symbols. Make this feel like a distinctive identity at first sight.

## Спина к спине

Make a single original pixel-perfect sportswear logotype lettering line with exactly "Sound Team" (uppercase S and T, all other letters lowercase). Wide 3:1 black landscape artboard, with the words on one line, centered with generous margins. Think of the distinctive weight and curved geometry of the classic Kappa Italian sports wordmark, rather than a videogame typeface. Design wide tightly kerned custom display letters, substantial but legible white strokes, generous open smooth bowl shapes, softened corners stepping crisply on a small square pixel grid, low horizontal proportions, gentle visual rhythm. Letterform features: tall round back S with a slightly flattened terminal, near-round o with an open bold counter, soft round bottom u, curved shoulder n, upright ascending d, broad flat cap T, distinctive rounded open e, single-storey a with a clear bowl and strong stem, rounded shoulder m. Clear visible space between words, forms separated and legible. Only white against black. No outlines, texture, artificial shadow, underline, decoration, emblems, or logo except these exact words. Do not draw all caps or make generic square block pixel text. Make it feel like a small counterpart to an iconic heavy rounded wordmark.
